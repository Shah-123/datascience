# Graduate and Research Studies

## Master's Degree Requirements
A master's degree requires at least 30 credits, except for the MS in Data Science, which requires 36 credits. Students must maintain a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0 to graduate. All requirements must be completed within 5 years of first enrollment. Master's students choose either a thesis option, which includes 6 thesis credits, or a non-thesis option that ends with a capstone project.

## Thesis Committee and Defense
A thesis committee has at least three members, and at least two of them must come from the student's home department. The committee must be formed by the end of the student's second term of thesis work.

The student submits the completed thesis to the committee at least 3 weeks before the defense, and the defense must be announced at least 10 business days in advance. A student passes when no more than one committee member votes against. The final electronic thesis must be uploaded to the Graduate School by the fourth Friday before commencement.

## Graduate Assistantships
Research Assistants receive a stipend of $22,000 for the 9-month academic year, and Teaching Assistants receive $18,500. Both work 20 hours per week. An assistantship includes a tuition waiver for up to 9 credits per term, but it does not cover mandatory fees.

Assistants must be enrolled full-time and keep a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0. Master's students may hold an assistantship for at most 4 terms.

## Human Subjects Research and the IRB
Research involving human participants must be approved by the Institutional Review Board (IRB) before any data is collected. Exempt reviews take about 10 business days, expedited reviews take about 4 weeks, and full-board reviews take 6 to 8 weeks because the board meets only on the second Tuesday of each month.

Every researcher must complete CITI human-subjects training, which stays valid for 3 years. Research data must be kept for 5 years after the project ends.

## Research Funding and Travel
The Graduate Research Fund provides up to $2,000 per student for research expenses. The conference travel grant covers up to $1,200 per year for students who are presenting an accepted paper. Travel grant applications must be submitted at least 30 days before the trip.

## Doctoral Milestones
Doctoral students must pass the qualifying examination by the end of their fourth term, and they may attempt it at most twice. After passing the qualifying examination and defending a dissertation prospectus, the student advances to candidacy. All doctoral requirements must be completed within 8 years of first enrollment.
