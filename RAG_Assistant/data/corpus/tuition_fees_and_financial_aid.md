# Tuition, Fees and Financial Aid

## Undergraduate and Graduate Tuition
For the 2026-27 academic year, undergraduate tuition is $1,150 per credit. Undergraduates who register for 12 to 18 credits pay a flat rate of $13,800 per term; credits above 18 are billed at $1,150 each, and part-time undergraduates (fewer than 12 credits) pay per credit.

Graduate tuition is $1,480 per credit. The MS in Data Science has a special rate of $1,650 per credit. Summer tuition is charged per credit at the same rate as the academic year. HRU makes no distinction between in-state and out-of-state students, and international students pay the same per-credit tuition as domestic students.

## Mandatory Fees
All degree-seeking students pay the following fees each term: a technology fee of $180, a student activity fee of $95, and a health services fee of $240. The health services fee cannot be waived. Fees are charged per term, not per credit.

Students enrolled in a laboratory course also pay a science lab fee of $120 per lab course. International students pay an International Student Services fee of $300 per term.

## Billing and Payment Deadlines
Fall term bills are issued on July 15 and are due August 15. Spring term bills are issued on December 15 and are due January 15. A late payment fee of $75 is charged when the balance is not paid in full by the due date, unless the student is enrolled in a payment plan.

The payment plan costs $40 per term and splits the balance into four equal installments due on the 15th of each month from August to November (fall) or from January to April (spring).

A financial hold is placed on any account whose unpaid balance exceeds $250. Accounts with a financial hold cannot register for courses or order transcripts.

## Tuition Refund Schedule
When a student drops or withdraws from courses, tuition is refunded according to the week of the term in which the change is processed.

| Week of term | Tuition refunded |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1-2 | 100% |
| Week 3 | 50% |
| Weeks 4-5 | 25% |
| Week 6 and later | 0% |

Mandatory fees are refundable only if the student withdraws during week 1. Refunds are issued within 14 business days to the original form of payment. Students called to active military service receive a 100% tuition refund regardless of the week.

Because the last day to withdraw with a W is later than the last day to receive a refund, an undergraduate who withdraws in week 6 or later receives a W on the transcript but no tuition refund.

## Merit Scholarships
The Provost Scholarship is worth $12,000 per year and is awarded to entering first-year students with an unweighted high school GPA of 3.8 or higher. The Ridge Scholar Award is worth $6,000 per year for entering first-year students with a GPA of at least 3.5 but below 3.8.

To renew the scholarship each year, Provost Scholars must keep a cumulative HRU GPA of at least 3.3 and Ridge Scholars a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0; both must complete at least 30 credits per academic year. Scholarships can be used for a maximum of 8 terms.

A scholar who falls below the renewal GPA is given one probationary term to recover. If the GPA is still below the requirement after that term, the scholarship is cancelled for the following term.

## Need-Based Aid and Work-Study
Students apply for need-based aid by filing the FAFSA; the priority deadline is February 15. Eligible students may receive the Ridge Grant, which is awarded on financial need alone.

Federal Work-Study is available to U.S. citizens and permanent residents only. It pays $16.50 per hour, and students may work at most 15 hours per week while classes are in session. Work-study earnings are paid biweekly and are not credited against tuition.
