# Housing and Campus Life

## Housing Application and Deposit
First-year students must live in university housing unless they live with a parent or guardian within 30 miles of campus. The housing application opens on March 1. A housing deposit of $400 is required to secure a room.

The housing deposit is fully refundable if the student cancels by June 1. Cancellations between June 2 and July 15 receive a 50% refund, and no refund is given after July 15. The housing deposit is separate from the enrollment deposit paid to the Office of Admissions.

## Room Types and Rates
Room rates are charged per term. A traditional double costs $4,200, a suite-style double costs $4,900, a single room costs $5,600, and an apartment costs $6,300. Room assignments are made in the order that completed applications and deposits are received, and students may request a roommate by mutual agreement.

## Meal Plans
The Ridge Unlimited plan costs $2,650 per term, the Block 150 plan (150 meals per term) costs $2,100, and the Block 75 plan costs $1,450. Commuter students may buy a 30-meal plan for $400 per term. First-year students living on campus must choose Ridge Unlimited or Block 150. A meal plan may be changed only during the first 10 days of a term.

## Residence Hall Rules
Quiet hours are 10 p.m. to 8 a.m. Sunday through Thursday and midnight to 10 a.m. on Friday and Saturday. Courtesy hours apply at all times.

Overnight guests may stay for at most 3 consecutive nights and no more than 6 nights per month, and every guest must be signed in by their host. Candles, hot plates and other open-flame or open-coil appliances are prohibited; cooking is allowed only in common kitchens. Alcohol is not permitted in first-year halls or for any resident under 21.

The only pets allowed are fish in tanks of up to 20 gallons. Service animals and approved emotional support animals are permitted with an accommodation letter from the Office of Accessibility Services.

## Parking Permits
A commuter permit for Lot C costs $320 per academic year, and a resident permit for Lot R costs $450 per academic year. Only students with sophomore standing or above may buy a resident permit. A motorcycle permit costs $90. Parking without a valid permit results in a $50 citation, which can be appealed within 14 days of issue.

## Lockouts, Keys and ID Cards
The first room lockout each term is free; each additional lockout costs $15. Replacing a lost room key requires a lock change and costs $150. The HRU One Card serves as student ID, meal card and door key, and a replacement card costs $25.

## Break Housing
Residence halls close 24 hours after a resident's last final examination in the fall term. Students who need to stay during winter break may petition for break housing, which costs $35 per night.
