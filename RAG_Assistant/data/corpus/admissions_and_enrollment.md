# Admissions and Enrollment

## Undergraduate Application Deadlines
First-year applicants choose between two deadlines. Early Action applications are due November 1, and decisions are released by December 15. Regular Decision applications are due January 15, and decisions are released by March 25. Transfer applicants for spring entry must apply by November 15.

Admitted students confirm their place by paying a non-refundable enrollment deposit of $300 on or before May 1. The undergraduate application fee is $60; applicants who qualify for a fee waiver pay nothing.

## Undergraduate Admission Requirements
Applicants submit a secondary school transcript, two letters of recommendation, and a personal essay of 500 to 650 words. HRU is test-optional: SAT and ACT scores are welcome but not required. A minimum unweighted high school GPA of 3.0 is recommended.

Applicants whose first language is not English must show English proficiency with a TOEFL iBT score of 85, an IELTS score of 6.5, or a Duolingo English Test score of 115.

## Graduate Admission
Graduate applicants apply directly to a program. Fall deadlines are December 1 (the priority deadline, required for assistantship consideration) and March 1 (the final deadline, with no funding priority). The spring deadline is October 15.

Applicants submit transcripts showing at least a 3.0 undergraduate GPA, three letters of recommendation, and a statement of purpose. The GRE is not required, with one exception: applicants to the MS in Data Science must submit GRE scores, and a Quantitative score of 160 or higher is expected. Graduate applicants whose first language is not English need a TOEFL iBT score of 90 or an IELTS score of 7.0.

## Transfer Credit
HRU accepts up to 60 credits from accredited two-year institutions and up to 75 credits in total from all external sources, including AP, IB and dual-enrollment credit. Only courses with a grade of C or better transfer, and transferred grades are not included in the HRU GPA. Transfer students must still satisfy the residency requirement described in the Graduation and Degree Requirements handbook.

Graduate students may transfer up to 9 credits with a grade of B or better, provided the courses were completed within the last 5 years and were not used for another degree.

## Deferral of Admission
An admitted first-year student may defer enrollment for one academic year by submitting a deferral request to the Office of Admissions by June 1. The enrollment deposit is retained and applied to the deferred term. Students who enroll at another degree-granting institution during the deferral year forfeit their place. A deferral cannot be extended beyond one year.

## Course Registration
Registration opens in order of class standing on successive days: seniors (90 or more earned credits) first, then juniors (60 to 89 credits), sophomores (30 to 59 credits), and first-year students (fewer than 30 credits) last. Graduate students register during the senior window.

Registration is blocked while the student has a financial hold, which is placed when the unpaid balance exceeds $250, or an immunization hold. Students clear a financial hold by paying the balance at the Bursar's Office or online through the student portal; the hold is lifted within one business day.

## Adding, Dropping and Withdrawing from Courses
Students may add a course through the end of week 1 without permission, and during week 2 with the instructor's permission. A late registration fee of $50 applies to anyone who registers on or after the first day of classes.

A course dropped through the end of week 2 does not appear on the transcript. Undergraduates who withdraw from a course between week 3 and the end of week 10 receive a grade of W. Graduate students may withdraw with a W between week 3 and the end of week 12. A W does not affect the GPA.

After these deadlines a withdrawal is possible only through a medical or emergency petition. The compressed 8-week summer term follows a shorter calendar: courses may be dropped without a record through the end of week 1, and a W is available through the end of week 5.
