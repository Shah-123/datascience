# Library and IT Services

## Borrowing Privileges
Undergraduates may borrow up to 25 items at a time for 28 days each, and may renew an item twice. Graduate students may borrow up to 50 items for 60 days each, with three renewals. DVDs circulate for 7 days. Laptops are loaned for 3 days and cannot be renewed. Course reserve items may be used inside the library for 2 hours at a time.

## Overdue Fines and Lost Items
Overdue books accrue a fine of $0.40 per day per item, capped at $25 per item. Overdue course reserve items cost $5 per hour, and an overdue laptop costs $10 per day. A lost item is charged at its replacement cost plus a $15 processing fee. The library blocks a student's borrowing account when unpaid fines reach $50.

## Interlibrary Loan
Interlibrary loan is free for students, who may submit up to 20 requests per term. Requests usually arrive in 3 to 7 business days, and borrowed items are loaned for 21 days.

## Study Rooms and Library Hours
Group study rooms can be booked for up to 3 hours per day, up to 14 days in advance. A room is released if nobody arrives within 15 minutes of the start time.

The library is open Monday to Thursday from 7 a.m. to 1 a.m., Friday from 7 a.m. to 9 p.m., Saturday from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Sunday from noon to 1 a.m. During reading week and final exam week the library is open 24 hours a day.

## Accounts, Wi-Fi and Passwords
Every student receives an HRU network account. Students connect to the HRU-Secure Wi-Fi network with their account credentials, and may register up to 5 devices. Visitors use HRU-Guest, which issues a 24-hour pass. Passwords expire every 180 days, and multi-factor authentication is required for all accounts.

Passwords can be reset in the self-service portal, or in person at the IT Help Desk in Turing Hall, Room 110, which is open from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays.

## Printing
Each student receives a free allowance of 500 pages per term, which resets at the start of each term. After the allowance is used, black-and-white pages cost $0.08 each and color pages cost $0.30 each. 3D printing in the Makerspace requires a one-hour safety training.

## Cloud Storage and Software
Students receive 1 TB of storage in HRU Cloud Drive and free access to Microsoft 365, MATLAB and Anaconda. Cloud Drive files are deleted 12 months after a student leaves the university, so graduates should back up their files. Student email addresses remain active for life as forwarding addresses.
