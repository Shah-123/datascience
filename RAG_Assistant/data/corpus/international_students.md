# International Student Handbook

## Full-Time Enrollment Requirement
Students on an F-1 visa must maintain full-time enrollment in every fall and spring term: at least 12 credits for undergraduates and at least 9 credits for graduate students. Only one online course, worth up to 3 credits, may count toward the full-time requirement in a term.

A student who needs to drop below full-time must obtain a Reduced Course Load (RCL) authorization from the ISSO before the drop is processed, and must request it at least 10 business days in advance. Dropping below full-time without authorization can end the student's legal status.

## Arrival and Check-In
New international students must check in with the ISSO within 15 days of the program start date printed on the I-20. The ISSO confirms the student's passport, visa and I-20, and updates the student's U.S. address in the SEVIS system. International Orientation is mandatory and takes place over three days before general orientation begins.

## On-Campus Employment
F-1 students may work on campus for up to 20 hours per week while classes are in session, and full-time during official breaks. A student needs a Social Security Number to be paid. International students are not eligible for Federal Work-Study.

## Curricular Practical Training (CPT)
Curricular Practical Training lets students work off campus in an internship that is an integral part of their program. Undergraduates become eligible after two consecutive terms of full-time study, while graduate students in programs that require an internship may use CPT from their first term.

CPT requires ISSO approval and enrollment in a 1-credit internship course. Students must apply at least 15 business days before the start date. Twelve months or more of full-time CPT makes a student ineligible for Optional Practical Training.

## Optional Practical Training (OPT)
Optional Practical Training gives F-1 graduates up to 12 months of work authorization in their field of study. Graduates of designated STEM degrees may apply for a 24-month extension. Students may apply up to 90 days before their program end date and no later than 60 days after it.

## Health Insurance for International Students
All international students are enrolled automatically in the Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP). Unlike domestic students, international students can waive SHIP only by submitting a private plan that offers at least $250,000 in coverage per accident or illness and $50,000 for medical repatriation. Waiver requests are decided by the ISSO.

## Travel and Visa Documents
Before international travel, students need a travel signature on the I-20. A signature is valid for 12 months, or 6 months for students on OPT. Students should apply for a visa renewal at least 3 months before the visa expires and should carry their passport, I-20 and proof of enrollment when re-entering the country.

The International Student Services fee that supports ISSO programs is described in the Tuition, Fees and Financial Aid handbook.
