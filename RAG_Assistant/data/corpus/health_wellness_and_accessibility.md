# Health, Wellness and Accessibility

## Student Health Center
The Student Health Center is open Monday to Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. and accepts walk-ins. The first 6 visits each term are covered by the health services fee; each later visit costs $25. A nurse line answers questions 24 hours a day, including weekends.

## Student Health Insurance Plan
Domestic students are enrolled automatically in the Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP), which costs $1,900 per year. A student who already has comparable coverage may waive SHIP by submitting proof of insurance by August 15 for the fall term or January 15 for the spring term. Waiver decisions are made within 10 business days. Special waiver rules apply to international students and are described in the International Student Handbook.

## Immunization Requirements
All students must submit proof of two doses of the MMR vaccine. Students who live in university housing must also show proof of the meningococcal vaccine. Records must be uploaded to the health portal by July 1; students whose records are missing after that date receive an immunization hold that blocks registration.

## Counseling Services
Each student receives 8 free individual counseling sessions per academic year, and each additional session costs $20. Group therapy is always free. Crisis walk-in appointments are available on weekdays during business hours, and the 24-hour crisis line is available at 555-0142.

## Accessibility Accommodations
Students who need disability accommodations register with the Office of Accessibility Services (OAS) and provide documentation from a licensed professional. Approved accommodations may include extended time on exams (usually 1.5 times the standard time), a distraction-reduced testing room, and note-taking support.

Exam accommodations must be requested at least 5 business days before each regular exam and at least 10 business days before final examinations. Accommodations are not retroactive and apply only from the date the student's accommodation letter is delivered to instructors.

## Medical Leave of Absence
A student may take a medical leave of absence of up to two consecutive terms with a letter from a licensed healthcare provider. The request must be submitted before the last day to withdraw, which is the end of week 10 for undergraduates.

Students on approved medical leave receive a W in all courses. Tuition is refunded at 100% through week 5 and at 50% for weeks 6 to 10, which is more generous than the standard refund schedule. To return, the student must submit a clearance letter from their provider at least 30 days before the start of the term.

## Campus Safety
Campus Safety is staffed 24 hours a day and can be reached at 555-0100. Blue-light emergency phones are located throughout campus. The free Safe-Ride shuttle runs every night from 6 p.m. to 2 a.m. Students receive emergency notices by text through the HRU Alert system.
