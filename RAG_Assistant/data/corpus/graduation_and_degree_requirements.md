# Graduation and Degree Requirements

## Bachelor's Degree Requirements
A bachelor's degree requires at least 120 credits, including the 42-credit General Education core and at least 39 upper-division credits (courses numbered 300 and above). Students must have a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 and a GPA of at least 2.3 in courses within their major.

## Residency Requirement
To earn an HRU degree, a student must complete at least 45 credits at HRU, and at least 24 of the last 30 credits before graduation must be taken at HRU. Transfer credit counts toward the 120 credits but not toward the residency requirement.

## Senior Capstone
Every undergraduate completes a 3-credit senior capstone course, numbered 490, in the final year of study. The capstone must be taken in the student's major, and it cannot be satisfied with transfer credit.

## Applying to Graduate
Students apply to graduate through the student portal by February 1 for May graduation, June 1 for August graduation, or October 1 for December graduation. A late application is accepted for up to 3 weeks after the deadline and carries a $50 late fee. The graduation application fee is $95.

Commencement is held once a year, in May. December graduates may take part in the following May ceremony. August graduates may walk in the preceding May ceremony if they need no more than 6 credits to finish and are registered for them.

## Latin Honors
Latin honors are awarded on the basis of the cumulative GPA at graduation: cum laude for 3.50 or higher, magna cum laude for 3.70 or higher, and summa cum laude for 3.90 or higher. A student must have earned at least 60 credits at HRU to be eligible for Latin honors.

## Majors and Minors
A minor requires 18 credits, of which at least 9 must be upper-division. A second major requires at least 24 additional credits that are not counted toward the first major. Students may declare at most two majors and two minors, and up to 6 credits may be counted toward both a major and a minor.

## Diplomas and Transcripts
Diplomas are mailed within 8 weeks after the degree is conferred. Requests to change the name printed on a diploma require legal documentation of the change.

An official electronic transcript costs $8 and a printed transcript costs $10. Transcripts are processed within 2 business days, and they cannot be issued to a student with a financial hold.
